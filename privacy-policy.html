⚡ AI Context Switcher

Privacy Policy

🔒 Zero Data Collection

1. Introduction

AI Context Switcher ("the Extension") is a Chrome browser extension that allows users to switch their AI chat conversation context between platforms like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Extension accesses, how it is used, and your rights.

2. Data Collection

We do NOT collect any personal data.

The Extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, browsing history, or user data to any external servers, third-party services, or any party whatsoever.

3. Data Storage

The Extension stores the following data locally on your device only using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.local API:

  • Chat context: Temporarily stores the conversation messages you choose to capture from an AI platform, so they can be pasted into another AI platform.
  • Switch history: A local log of your context switches for your convenience (up to 50 entries).
  • Pending switch flag: A temporary flag indicating which platform to switch to (auto-deleted after use).

All of this data is stored entirely on your local device. It is never transmitted anywhere.

4. Website Access

The Extension requires access to the following websites solely to extract and inject chat context:

  • chatgpt.com / chat.openai.com (ChatGPT)
  • claude.ai (Claude by Anthropic)
  • gemini.google.com (Google Gemini)
  • chat.deepseek.com (DeepSeek)
  • perplexity.ai (Perplexity AI)
  • copilot.microsoft.com (Microsoft Copilot)

The Extension only reads visible chat content from these pages when you explicitly trigger a context capture. It does not access any other data on these websites.

5. Permissions Used

  • storage: To save captured chat context locally on your device.
  • activeTab: To access the currently active tab when you click the extension.
  • tabs: To open new tabs when switching to a target AI platform.
  • scripting: To inject content scripts into AI chat pages for context extraction.

6. Third-Party Services

The Extension does NOT use any third-party services, analytics, tracking tools, advertising networks, or external APIs. There are zero network requests made by the Extension.

7. Data Sharing

We do NOT share, sell, rent, or trade any user data with any third parties. Period.

8. Data Retention

Chat context data is stored locally until you clear it manually or uninstall the Extension. Uninstalling the Extension automatically removes all locally stored data.

9. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.
